# diabetes-sentiment-analysis
This project analyzes public sentiment and keyword trends in Reddit posts related to diabetes. Using Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques, it identifies emotional patterns, common discussion topics, and community perceptions around diabetes management, treatment, and lifestyle.

The analysis combines sentiment classification, keyword frequency extraction, and interactive exploration of posts to provide insights into how people discuss diabetes online.
## Objectives
- Quantify sentiment distribution (positive, negative, neutral) in diabetes-related Reddit posts.
- Identify the most frequent keywords and their sentiment associations.
- Visualize patterns to understand community concerns and emotional tone.
- Enable interactive exploration of posts by keyword and sentiment.
